How to play
  • Each side starts with 150 points. The first to reach 0 wins.
  • Type a word that starts with the last letter of the previous word. At least 4 letters, and no repeats.
  • A word costs the seconds left on your clock (rounded) plus its letters beyond 4. Faster words cost more, so think fast.
  • Run out of time and you pass: your score goes up by 5 and the next player gets the same letter.
  • Four passes in a row is a stalemate.
