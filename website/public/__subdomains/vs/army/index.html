Spacing is the density control: it adds transparent pixels after each tile (0 = packed as your PNG is), and the loop rule then applies to the padded tile. For emoji that sit side by side in Slack, make the canvas a multiple of the (padded) tile width, or the pattern breaks at each emoji edge. The square preset resamples neocat to 42×42 so stacked emoji line up too. Tight-crop your sprite first. The PNG itself is the repeating tile, so it should tile seamlessly. Also, frames × |dx| must be a whole multiple of the tile width (and likewise for dy and height), or the loop will jump.
