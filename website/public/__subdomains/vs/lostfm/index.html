your own lostfm

Connect Spotify and this page keeps your history in this browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere). It syncs whenever the page is open; Spotify remembers your last 50 plays, so opening it about once a day keeps everything.

Spotify only lets people the app owner has added log in while the app is in development mode. If Spotify says you're not allowed, ask izie to add your Spotify email. You can still import a Last.fm export without connecting.
