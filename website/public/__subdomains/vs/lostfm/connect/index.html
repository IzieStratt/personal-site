Spotify

Reads what you play, live. Spotify only remembers your last 50 plays, so open this page about once a day to keep everything.

Connect through your own free Spotify app. It takes about 3 minutes, and you're automatically allowed in. Spotify limits each app to 5 people unless it's run by a company, so everyone brings their own. lostfm never sees a secret: your browser talks to Spotify directly, and the Client ID stays in this browser.

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard and log in.
  2. Give the app any name and description.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add
  4. Tick Web API, accept the terms and click Save.
  5. Copy the app's Client ID (not the secret) into the box below.

Apple Music & others

Get a private scrobble token, then point a scrobbler at lostfm. It speaks the ListenBrainz API, so most scrobblers work.

Apple Music on the web, YouTube Music, SoundCloud…
  1. Install the Web Scrobbler browser extension.
  2. Open its settings → Accounts → ListenBrainz, choose the custom option and paste the API URL and token above.
  3. Play something on music.apple.com.
The Music app on a Mac
  1. Download apple-music-scrobbler.py.
  2. In Terminal run python3 apple-music-scrobbler.py --token YOUR_TOKEN. Leave it running, or run it with --install to start it at login.
  3. macOS asks once to let Terminal control Music. Say OK.
Android, and iPhone
  1. Android: Pano Scrobbler → add a ListenBrainz account with a custom URL of https://vs.izie.top/lostfm/ and your token.
  2. iPhone: Apple doesn't let any app see live plays. Ask Apple for your data at privacy.apple.com and import the Apple Music Play Activity CSV below.

Import history

Bring your past plays. Importing the same file twice won't double-count.

Which files work?
  1. Last.fm: a CSV or JSON from lastfm.ghan.nl/export or lastfm-to-csv.
  2. Spotify: your Extended streaming history (the Streaming_History_Audio_*.json files) from Spotify's privacy page.
  3. Apple Music: Apple Music Play Activity.csv from privacy.apple.com.
  4. A lostfm download from this page.
