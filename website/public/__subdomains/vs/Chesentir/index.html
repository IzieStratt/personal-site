    05 / LORE

    On the meaning of "I WANT TO CHEESE."

    Summarised from a long essay by a member of the Hack Club Slack. Nobody has resolved the question. Everybody has a view.

    Five words, no punctuation.

    Zach Latta posted "I WANT TO CHEESE" in #zrl-land on the Hack Club Slack. No follow-up, no clarification. The community has been arguing about it ever since.

    Gaming, or the kitchen.

    In gaming, to cheese is to win by an unintended route. In a kitchen it means nothing at all. The message refuses to choose, and the refusal is the point.

    Universally legible.

    You don't need to know Hack Club lore, Haskell or what a PCB is. You only need to have wanted something once. The phrase is democratic in that way.

    Digital folk art.

    A small piece of folk art made from a Slack message. A reminder that even the most accomplished people are just people in a channel with needs that defy categorisation.

    “Zach wanted to cheese. We should all be so honest.” — the closing line of the essay, quoted in full. :3

    Interpretation spread (community survey, simulated) n = 4,812

    Gaming 41% · Kitchen 0% · Refuses to specify 59%. The third bucket is the one the essay is about.
