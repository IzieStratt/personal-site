Privacy

vs.izie.top is a personal site. It shows no ads, sets no advertising or tracking cookies and loads no analytics scripts. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Pages that store nothing

Most projects here, including ReDocumented, DeGoogledDict, the emoji tools and the parody pages, run entirely in your browser or only read public data. Some remember settings such as the theme in your own browser storage, which never leaves your device. DeGoogledDict sends the word you look up to public sources (Wiktionary, Merriam-Webster, Datamuse, Google Books Ngram, Wikipedia) to fetch its results. ReDocumented's API counts requests per IP address in memory for a minute to apply its rate limit and keeps no log of them.

Apps that store data

  • vibetime stores the account and activity data you choose to sync, so it can show your totals.
  • lostfm stores listens you send with a scrobble token; it keeps only a hash of the token.
  • The Shiritori game stores word suggestions you submit.

This data is kept on Cloudflare (Durable Objects and D1), used only to run that app, and never sold or shared.

Your requests

To ask what is stored about you, or to have it deleted, email ReDocumented@izie.top. See the contact page for other ways to reach me.
